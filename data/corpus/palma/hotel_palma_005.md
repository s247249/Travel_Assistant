## Description
Hotel Puerto Azul is a mid-range, family-friendly hotel near the port side of Palma de Mallorca, with sea-view rooms looking over the masts and the bay. Dedicated family rooms sleep up to four, the pool anchors the afternoon, and a solid included breakfast handles the morning rush before beach or boat. Air conditioning and wifi run throughout, and the tone is cheerful and practical.

## Who it's for
Hotel Puerto Azul is aimed squarely at families visiting Palma de Mallorca, and also suits groups of young friends sharing rooms and relax-seekers who want sea views at moderate cost. Four-person rooms make it one of the simplest family choices in the city.

## Practical notes
Costs are moderate, with family rooms offering strong per-person value. The port area of Palma de Mallorca puts catamaran departures and the waterfront promenade within walking distance; the old town is a stroll or short ride away. Request high-floor sea views when booking.
