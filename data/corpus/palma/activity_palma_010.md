## Description
Es Baluard Modern Art Museum is the contemporary counterweight to the Gothic weight of Palma de Mallorca. Built into and around the Renaissance Sant Pere bastion — part of the city's sixteenth-century sea walls — the museum itself is half the exhibit: raw sandstone ramparts, a vaulted cistern-like hall, and crisp modern galleries stacked above them, all crowned by a terrace that frames the cathedral and the bay in a single view. The collection focuses on modern and contemporary art with a strong Mallorcan and Mediterranean thread, anchored by artists who loved the island, from Sorolla and Miró to Barceló, alongside rotating exhibitions of photography and current practice. Two hours is enough to see the permanent collection, the current show and the terrace without rushing. Es Baluard is the most rewarding single indoor stop in Palma de Mallorca, and its architecture alone justifies the visit.

## Who it's for
Es Baluard suits solo travellers, couples and seniors who enjoy art at a contemplative pace. Lifts connect the levels of the bastion, benches are generous, and the air-conditioned galleries make it a strategic retreat on a hot Palma de Mallorca afternoon.

## Practical notes
The museum opens daily in season and booking is not required for individual visits; costs are low. Es Baluard sits at the western edge of the old town of Palma de Mallorca, a short walk from Santa Catalina, so it pairs neatly with the wine and tapas district for lunch afterward. The terrace view is best in late-afternoon light, and photography of the ramparts is encouraged.
