## Description
Hostal Casco Antiguo is a simple, well-kept budget hostal tucked into the old town of Palma de Mallorca, a few cobbled lanes from the cathedral. Rooms are compact and bright, with air conditioning and reliable wifi — the essentials done properly, without frills. The location is its luxury: mornings start among artisan shops and café bars, and most sights of Palma de Mallorca are on foot.

## Who it's for
Hostal Casco Antiguo in Palma de Mallorca suits solo travellers, young visitors and couples who spend their days out exploring and want a cheap, central base. Rooms sleep up to two guests, so it is not aimed at families needing extra beds.

## Practical notes
There is no restaurant or pool at Hostal Casco Antiguo; breakfast is easy in the surrounding old-town cafés of Palma de Mallorca. Costs are low. Expect some street noise in high season and pack light, as older buildings mean stairs and no lift.
