## Description
The Formentor Cape & Beach Day Trip takes visitors from Palma de Mallorca to the island's most photographed headland, a full seven-hour excursion to the far tip of the Serra de Tramuntana. The route crosses the plain of vineyards and almond groves, climbs through the pine-hung curves above Puerto Pollença, and follows the legendary cape road along knife-edge ridges with drops to impossibly blue water on both sides. At the lighthouse viewpoint the Mediterranean opens in three directions at once. The day balances the drama with stillness: a long stop at Formentor beach, a sheltered horseshoe of white sand and shallow, gin-clear water framed by pines, where swimming, paddling and a picnic lunch fill the early afternoon before the return run to Palma de Mallorca. It is the definitive contrast to the city — same island, wilder register.

## Who it's for
This day trip from Palma de Mallorca works for families, couples, sporty travellers and young explorers. The beach is shallow and gentle for children, the viewpoints need no walking effort, and photographers of every level come home with their best frames of the trip.

## Practical notes
The Formentor Cape & Beach Day Trip runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays, departing from Palma de Mallorca; booking ahead is recommended in summer. Access rules for private cars on the cape road change seasonally, so an organised excursion is the simplest option. Bring swimwear, towels, snorkels, sunscreen and water. The cost level is medium. Expect winding roads; motion-sensitive travellers should sit forward.
