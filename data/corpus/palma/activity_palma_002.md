## Description
The Catamaran Cove & Snorkel Cruise in Palma de Mallorca turns a full afternoon into an easygoing Mediterranean escape. Departing from the marinas along the Palma waterfront, the catamaran glides west along the island's southern coast, past the cliffs of Cala Major and Illetes, before anchoring in a sheltered turquoise cove reachable only by boat. Guests swim straight from the deck, snorkel over seagrass meadows alive with small fish, or simply stretch out on the nets and sun decks while the crew keeps drinks and a light onboard meal flowing. The five-hour format leaves room for two swimming stops when conditions allow, and the sail back toward Palma de Mallorca in the late afternoon light is a highlight in itself, with the cathedral silhouette growing slowly on the horizon.

## Who it's for
This Palma de Mallorca catamaran cruise is ideal for families with confident swimmers, groups of young travellers, couples and dedicated relax-seekers. Snorkelling gear is provided, life jackets are available for children, and the stable twin-hull deck suits nervous sailors better than a monohull. Non-swimmers can happily stay aboard and sunbathe.

## Practical notes
The Catamaran Cove & Snorkel Cruise runs daily from Palma de Mallorca in the warm season, and advance booking is strongly recommended because deck space is limited. Bring swimwear worn under light clothing, reef-friendly sunscreen, a hat and a towel. This is a premium-priced outing; the cost level is high, reflecting the crewed boat, equipment and onboard catering. Motion-sensitive guests should choose midship seating and calm-weather days.
