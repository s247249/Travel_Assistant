## Description
The Sunset Sailing Trip is Palma de Mallorca at its most cinematic. Leaving the city's marinas in the late afternoon, a crewed sailboat works out into the Badia de Palma under sail, engines off whenever the breeze allows, with the skyline of Palma de Mallorca sliding past: the cathedral above its walls, the terraced rooftops, the long arc of the waterfront. As the sun drops toward the western headlands the crew serves a chilled drink and light snacks, and the boat holds a comfortable position for the golden hour, when the sea turns copper and the Tramuntana ridges layer into silhouettes. Three hours is exactly enough to unwind, take the photographs, and glide back into port as the marina lights come on. It distils the relaxed, sea-facing character of Palma de Mallorca into a single evening.

## Who it's for
This sunset sail from Palma de Mallorca is made for couples, young travellers marking an occasion, and relax-seekers who want the sea without effort. No sailing experience is needed; guests can help at the lines or simply lounge. It is not aimed at families with toddlers, given the timing and open deck.

## Practical notes
The Sunset Sailing Trip runs daily in season from Palma de Mallorca, and booking ahead is recommended because guest numbers per boat are small. The cost level is high, reflecting a skippered yacht with drinks included. Bring a light layer for the return leg, when the breeze freshens, and non-slip shoes. Photographers should protect gear from spray; the best light comes in the final hour.
