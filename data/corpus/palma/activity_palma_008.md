## Description
The Mountain E-Bike Tour lets visitors cover the wilder edge of Palma de Mallorca in four active hours. Riding quality electric mountain bikes, a small group follows a guide out of the city along the coastal bluffs and into the foothills above the bay, mixing sea-view tracks, pine-forest trails and quiet country lanes toward old estates and clifftop viewpoints. The electric assist flattens the climbs without removing the effort, so riders choose how hard to work while still reaching terrain a regular bike would make punishing in summer. Stops come at a rocky cove for photos, a shaded ridge above the Badia de Palma, and a village fountain square where the guide unpacks the island's cycling culture — Mallorca is a world pilgrimage for road cyclists, and Palma de Mallorca is its natural base camp.

## Who it's for
This e-bike tour around Palma de Mallorca fits sporty travellers, young adventurers and solo visitors who prefer doing to sightseeing. Basic bike-handling confidence is required for gravel sections; no elite fitness is needed thanks to the assist. It is not suitable for young children or non-riders.

## Practical notes
The Mountain E-Bike Tour operates on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays from Palma de Mallorca, with booking recommended to reserve bikes and keep groups small. Helmets are provided; wear closed shoes, sportswear and sunscreen, and carry water. The cost level is medium and includes bike, gear and guiding. Morning departures avoid the fiercest afternoon heat in high summer, and the ride finishes near the waterfront of Palma de Mallorca.
