## Description
Hotel Cala Mayor sits above the Cala Major area west of central Palma de Mallorca, a relaxed coastal pocket with its own small beach. The mid-range hotel makes the most of its setting: sea-view rooms look across the bay, a pool terrace catches the sun through the afternoon, and breakfast is served with a horizon. Air conditioning and wifi come as standard, and the mood is unhurried rather than resort-loud.

## Who it's for
Hotel Cala Mayor in Palma de Mallorca suits couples, relax-seekers and small families; rooms take up to three guests. It fits travellers who want sea and pool on the doorstep without paying premium rates or staying in the busiest beach strip.

## Practical notes
Costs are moderate. Central Palma de Mallorca is a short bus ride away along the coast road, so culture mornings and pool afternoons combine easily. Sea-view rooms are worth requesting ahead in summer, and the walk down to the cove involves some steps.
