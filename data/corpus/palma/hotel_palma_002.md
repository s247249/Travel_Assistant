## Description
Casa Santa Catalina is a small budget guesthouse in Santa Catalina, the food-loving former fishermen's quarter of Palma de Mallorca. A simple included breakfast — bread, local cheese, fruit and good coffee — gets the day going before the market hall a short walk away. Rooms are modest but comfortable, with air conditioning and wifi throughout, and the neighbourhood's tapas bars stand in for any hotel restaurant.

## Who it's for
Casa Santa Catalina suits foodies, couples and solo travellers who want to live where Palma de Mallorca eats. Rooms accommodate up to two guests, making it a poor fit for families but ideal for pairs and independent explorers.

## Practical notes
Costs at Casa Santa Catalina are low, and the included breakfast improves the value further. The location in Palma de Mallorca is walkable to the old town and a short ride from the beaches; evenings in Santa Catalina are lively, so light sleepers should request a rear-facing room.
