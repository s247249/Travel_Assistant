## Description
The Santa Catalina Wine & Tapas Tasting explores the most flavoursome neighbourhood of Palma de Mallorca. Santa Catalina, the old fishermen's district west of the centre, has evolved into the city's dining quarter while keeping its market heart: a handsome iron-and-glass market hall where island producers sell sobrasada, cheeses, olives and just-landed fish. Over two and a half hours a host walks the market and surrounding lanes, stopping at beloved bars for guided tastings: Mallorcan wines from the Binissalem and Pla i Llevant appellations, crisp island whites and herbal local spirits, each paired with tapas that tell the island's story — pan amb oli rubbed with ramallet tomatoes, sobrasada with honey, and a modern bite or two from the district's newer kitchens. Between stops, the host sketches how Santa Catalina went from nets to napkins without losing its soul, making this the tastiest history lesson in Palma de Mallorca.

## Who it's for
This tasting walk in Palma de Mallorca is aimed at foodies, couples who travel to eat, and young travellers who like their culture with a glass in hand. Portions across the stops amount to a light meal. Vegetarian adaptations are usually possible with notice.

## Practical notes
The Santa Catalina Wine & Tapas Tasting runs on Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, and booking ahead is recommended for the small-group format. Come hungry but not starving; wear comfortable shoes for short walks between bars. The cost level is medium and covers all tastings. Evenings are liveliest; earlier departures are calmer and better for market atmosphere in Palma de Mallorca.
