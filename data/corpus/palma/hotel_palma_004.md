## Description
Residencia Badia de Palma is a comfortable mid-range residence-style hotel on the quieter residential side of the bay of Palma de Mallorca. Its strengths are practical: a good-sized pool with loungers, an included breakfast that suits early starts, air-conditioned rooms, and dependable wifi. The atmosphere is calm and neighbourly, closer to a guest apartment with hotel services than to a busy resort block.

## Who it's for
Residencia Badia de Palma suits families, seniors and relax-seekers who value quiet nights and easy days. Rooms sleep up to three guests. It works well for longer stays in Palma de Mallorca, when a pool base and a steady routine matter more than nightlife.

## Practical notes
Costs are moderate. The hotel is not in the walking heart of Palma de Mallorca, so plan on buses or short taxi rides for the old town and beaches; the flip side is calmer streets and easier evenings. Book pool-facing rooms early for summer stays.
