## Description
The Serra de Tramuntana Guided Hike from Palma de Mallorca trades the beach for the island's dramatic mountain spine, a UNESCO-listed cultural landscape of dry-stone terraces, olive groves and limestone peaks. A local guide leads a five-hour route on the lower slopes above the Palma basin, climbing through holm-oak forest and past old snow-houses toward panoramic viewpoints where the whole Badia de Palma opens below, from the cathedral spires to the curve of Playa de Palma. The pace is steady rather than racing, with pauses to explain the centuries-old water channels, terraced farming and the historic trail network that laces these hills. Returning to Palma de Mallorca by early afternoon leaves the evening free for a swim or a long dinner in the old town.

## Who it's for
This guided hike near Palma de Mallorca is built for sporty travellers, active young visitors and solo hikers who want mountain scenery without logistics. A reasonable base fitness level is needed for sustained ascent on rocky paths; it is not suited to small children or anyone with knee problems on descents.

## Practical notes
The Serra de Tramuntana Guided Hike operates on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays, and booking ahead is recommended as group sizes are kept small. Wear proper hiking shoes, carry more water than you think you need, and bring sun protection even under cloud. The cost level is medium and includes guiding and transfers from Palma de Mallorca. Summer departures start early to finish before the strongest heat.
