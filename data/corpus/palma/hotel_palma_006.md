## Description
Grand Hotel Miramar Palma is the city's polished high-tier address, set along the seafront promenade of Palma de Mallorca with sweeping bay views. Rooms and suites are spacious, family rooms sleep up to four, and the wellness floor — spa circuits, treatments and a serene pool area — is the reason many guests never quite leave. Breakfast is a generous spread with island produce, service is attentive without ceremony, and the cathedral skyline glitters across the water at dusk.

## Who it's for
Grand Hotel Miramar Palma suits couples on a treat, families wanting space and facilities together, and serious relax-seekers. It is the premium choice in Palma de Mallorca for travellers who want the sea, the spa and the city in one stay.

## Practical notes
Costs are high, in line with the tier. The seafront location means the old town of Palma de Mallorca is a pleasant promenade walk away. Spa slots and family rooms should be reserved well ahead for August.
