## Description

The Appian Way E-Bike Ride follows the Via Appia Antica, the ancient Roman road once called the queen of roads, on a guided electric-bike loop lasting about four hours. Leaving the city walls behind, you pedal past umbrella pines, catacomb entrances, ruined tombs and stretches of original basalt paving, with the open Roman countryside stretching toward the Castelli hills. The e-bike's assist makes the gently rolling terrain accessible without demanding race fitness, and regular stops let your guide unpack two thousand years of history. The ride operates on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays at a medium cost level that includes bike, helmet and guiding.

## Who it's for

Sporty travellers who prefer active sightseeing to museum queues are the natural audience, along with young travellers and adventurous couples. Confident older riders comfortable on a bike will also enjoy it. It is not designed for young children or anyone unable to ride a bicycle independently.

## Practical notes

Reserve ahead, especially for June–September 2026 slots in Rome, and choose the earliest departure to beat the heat. Bring water, sunscreen and closed shoes; some sections run on uneven ancient stones. Sunday traffic restrictions on the Appian Way do not apply to the ride's operating days, so expect occasional vehicles on shared stretches.
