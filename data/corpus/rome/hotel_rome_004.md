## Description

Residenza Borghese is a polished mid-tier hotel near the Villa Borghese gardens, on the elegant northern edge of central Rome. Rooms are calm and contemporary, with air conditioning, strong wifi and a breakfast spread that runs from yoghurt and fruit to eggs and cured meats. A compact but well-equipped gym lets guests keep training routines alive between sightseeing days. The location trades postcard views for something arguably better: morning runs under the park's pines, the Borghese Gallery minutes away, and the Spanish Steps a pleasant walk downhill.

## Who it's for

Sporty travellers who want a gym and green running space, couples seeking a refined but not extravagant base, and solo business-leisure visitors will appreciate the setup. Rooms sleep a maximum of two, so this is a pairs-and-singles address rather than a family one.

## Practical notes

The area is quieter at night than the historic core, which most guests count as a feature; dinner means a walk or short ride toward the centre. The nearby Spagna metro stop links you to the Termini hub. Reserve early for summer 2026 stays in Rome.
