## Description

The Pizza & Gelato Making Class is a hands-on three-hour session in a teaching kitchen in central Rome, where a friendly local instructor walks you through two Italian icons. First you learn the science of a proper pizza dough, stretch your own base and dress it with classic toppings before sliding it into a hot oven. Then the group shifts to gelato, churning a batch from scratch and learning what separates authentic artisan gelato from industrial ice cream. The class runs on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at a medium cost level, and you finish by eating everything you made — lunch or dessert solved.

## Who it's for

Families with children old enough to knead and stir get a memorable shared activity, young travellers enjoy the social, slightly messy fun, and couples can turn it into a playful date. Foodies gain techniques worth reproducing at home. The indoor, air-conditioned kitchen also makes this a smart pick on Rome's hottest afternoons.

## Practical notes

Book ahead, as class sizes in Rome are capped so everyone gets counter space. Mention allergies or dietary needs when reserving; gluten-free adaptations may be limited. Wear washable clothing and tie back long hair. Recipes and tips are shared, so you leave with skills as well as a full stomach.
