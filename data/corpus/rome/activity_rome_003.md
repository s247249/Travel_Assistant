## Description

The Trastevere Evening Food Tour winds through the ivy-draped lanes of Trastevere, Rome's most atmospheric dining quarter, over about three leisurely hours. A local host leads a small group from a family-run pizzeria to a historic deli, an artisan gelateria and a neighbourhood enoteca, pairing tastings of supplì, cured meats, Roman-style pizza al taglio and regional wines with stories about the district's working-class roots and nightlife rebirth. The tour runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, and its high cost level covers generous tastings that comfortably replace dinner. Between stops you stroll cobbled streets around Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere as the evening lights come on.

## Who it's for

Foodies who want to eat like Romans rather than tourists will get the most from this evening. It also suits couples looking for a sociable date-night experience, young travellers who enjoy meeting other guests, and solo diners who prefer company at the table. Come hungry and unhurried.

## Practical notes

Book ahead, as groups in Rome are deliberately small and weekend dates fill first. Wear comfortable shoes for cobblestones. Tell your host about dietary restrictions when reserving; vegetarian adaptations are usually possible, but the itinerary is built around classic Roman flavours.
