## Description

The Catacombs of San Callisto Visit descends into the largest of Rome's early Christian underground cemeteries, along the Appian Way southeast of the city centre. For about ninety minutes, a guided route follows narrow tufa corridors lined with tiered burial niches, leading past frescoed chambers and the Crypt of the Popes, where several third-century pontiffs were laid to rest. The low cost level makes this one of Rome's most evocative budget cultural stops, and visits run daily. Below ground the air stays cool and constant, a striking contrast to the sunlit Roman countryside above the entrance.

## Who it's for

History-minded seniors, solo travellers and couples who like their archaeology atmospheric will find this deeply rewarding. Families with school-age children fascinated by hidden tunnels also do well; the confined corridors are less suitable for strollers or anyone uncomfortable underground.

## Practical notes

Visits are guided only, so book or arrive ready to join the next available group, particularly in busy summer 2026 periods. The temperature underground is noticeably cool and damp: carry a light layer even in August. Steps and low ceilings are part of the route. Combine the visit with an Appian Way walk or ride while you are already outside the walls.
