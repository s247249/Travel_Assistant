## Description

Hotel Trastevere Verde sits on a quieter lane at the edge of Trastevere, Rome's favourite evening dining quarter, with a leafy internal courtyard that lives up to its name. The mid-tier property offers comfortable rooms with air conditioning, solid wifi and an included breakfast built around fresh pastries, fruit and proper espresso. Rooms accommodate up to three guests, making it a practical base for small families. Step outside and you are minutes from Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere, the Janiculum climb and a dense concentration of trattorias.

## Who it's for

Couples who want atmosphere on the doorstep, foodies planning to eat their way through the district, and families of three will all find the balance of comfort and location appealing. The medium cost level buys genuine convenience without luxury pricing.

## Practical notes

Trastevere has no metro station, so plan on trams, buses and walking to reach the Vatican or ancient centre. The neighbourhood is lively at night; courtyard-facing rooms are the quietest. Book well ahead for June–September 2026, when Rome's charming mid-range rooms disappear early.
