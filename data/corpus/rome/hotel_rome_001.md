## Description

B&B Aurelia is a small, family-run bed and breakfast in Rome's Prati district, on the Vatican side of the Tiber. Its handful of simply furnished rooms keep things quiet and personal, with a low cost level that leaves more budget for the city's tables and museums. A modest Italian breakfast of coffee, pastries and fruit is included each morning, and reliable wifi covers the whole house. The hosts are happy to sketch walking routes toward St Peter's and the river bridges into the historic centre.

## Who it's for

Couples and solo travellers watching their spending will feel at home, as will young travellers who value a local host's advice over hotel formality. Rooms sleep a maximum of two guests, so families needing extra beds should look elsewhere in Rome.

## Practical notes

As a budget property, expect stairs rather than lifts and compact room sizes. Air conditioning is not among the amenities, so summer guests from June to September should ask for the coolest-facing room and plan midday breaks elsewhere. Breakfast hours are fixed but generous enough for early sightseeing starts.
