## Description

The Borghese Gallery Timed Entry offers two focused hours inside the Villa Borghese Pinciana, a seventeenth-century cardinal's palace set in the green heart of Rome. The collection is compact but extraordinary: Bernini's marble sculptures, including Apollo and Daphne, appear to breathe, while Caravaggio's canvases anchor one of the finest painting collections in Italy. Canova, Raphael and Titian round out rooms decorated with frescoes and ancient mosaics. Because visits are organised in timed waves and capped in size, the gallery feels calm even in high season, and the cost level stays low. Entry slots are offered daily.

## Who it's for

This is a connoisseur's stop: couples who enjoy art without crowds, seniors who appreciate a manageable two-hour indoor visit with seating, and solo travellers drawn to Baroque Rome. Families with restless children may find the hushed rooms demanding; the surrounding gardens make a better release valve afterwards.

## Practical notes

Reservation is effectively essential in Rome, particularly for summer 2026, since each timed wave sells out. Arrive early to collect tickets and check bags, as large items must be stored. Combine the visit with a stroll in Villa Borghese park to stretch your legs before or after your slot.
