## Description

The Colosseum & Roman Forum Guided Tour is the classic introduction to ancient Rome. Over roughly three hours, a licensed guide leads you through the Flavian Amphitheatre, where gladiatorial games once drew tens of thousands of spectators, then continues along the Via Sacra into the Roman Forum, the political and religious heart of the ancient city. You will stand among the ruins of temples, basilicas and the Senate house while your guide reconstructs daily life in imperial Rome. The tour runs daily and its premium cost reflects expert guiding and coordinated entry to two of the most visited monuments in Italy.

## Who it's for

This tour suits families with older children who enjoy stories of gladiators and emperors, couples seeking a shared cultural highlight, and solo travellers who appreciate context they would miss alone. Seniors with reasonable mobility can manage the route, though the Forum's uneven ancient paving requires steady footing. It is less ideal for very young children on hot afternoons.

## Practical notes

Book well ahead, especially for June through September 2026, when demand in Rome peaks. The route is almost entirely outdoors and exposed: bring a hat, water and comfortable shoes. Mornings are cooler and quieter than midday. Large bags are not practical inside the monuments.
