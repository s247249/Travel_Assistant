## Description

Casa Navona puts you a short stroll from Piazza Navona, in the tangle of lanes that makes central Rome so addictive. This budget guesthouse occupies a floor of an old palazzo, with pared-back rooms, dependable wifi and — crucially for Roman summers — air conditioning in every room. The low cost level reflects a lean offering: there is no included breakfast, but the surrounding streets overflow with cafés where a cappuccino and cornetto cost little. Pantheon, Campo de' Fiori and the Tiber bridges are all comfortably walkable from the front door.

## Who it's for

Young travellers, couples and solo explorers who want location above luxury are the ideal guests. Sleeping a maximum of two per room, it suits pairs rather than families. Those who like to start the day in a neighbourhood bar rather than a hotel dining room will barely notice the missing breakfast.

## Practical notes

Central Rome's historic buildings often mean stairs and small lifts; pack light. Evening street noise comes with the location, so light sleepers should request an interior-facing room. In August, the air conditioning makes this one of the more sensible budget choices in the city centre.
