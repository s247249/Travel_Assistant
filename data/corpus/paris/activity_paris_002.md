## Description
Eiffel Tower Summit Access is the signature viewpoint experience of Paris. Built as the entrance arch for the 1889 World's Fair, the iron lattice tower was once controversial and is now the defining symbol of the French capital. This experience takes visitors up through the tower's levels to the summit, where an open-air platform offers a sweeping panorama of the entire city: the Seine curving under its bridges, the golden dome of Les Invalides, the white hilltop silhouette of Sacre-Coeur and the geometric avenues radiating from the Arc de Triomphe. Along the way, the intermediate floors provide close-up views of the riveted ironwork and the engineering that made the tower a marvel of its age. Allow around two and a half hours including lifts, viewing time and the inevitable photo stops.

## Who it's for
Summit access at the Eiffel Tower suits couples looking for a memorable Paris moment, families with children old enough to enjoy the height, and young travellers chasing the classic skyline photograph. Photographers of every level will be rewarded. Visitors uneasy with heights or crowded lift cabins should weigh the summit carefully; the lower floors already deliver superb views.

## Practical notes
This Paris experience operates daily, and reserving a summit slot in advance is strongly recommended because demand is high in summer. Weather matters: low cloud can hide the panorama, and the summit platform is exposed and windy even on warm days, so bring a light layer. Security checks and lift queues are part of the visit, so arrive with time to spare. Cost is moderate for an iconic Paris landmark.
