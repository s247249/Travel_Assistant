## Description
Hotel Tour Eiffel Vue is a mid-range hotel in the western districts of Paris, within easy reach of the Champ de Mars and the iron landmark that gives it its name. Rooms accommodate up to three guests, and the property pairs the essentials of wifi, breakfast and air conditioning with a gym, a rarity at this tier in Paris. Mornings can start with a workout or a walk to the tower before the crowds build, and the neighbouring streets offer markets, bistros and river quays for evening strolls. It is a practical, comfortable base for travellers who want activity and icons in equal measure.

## Who it's for
This Paris hotel suits sporty travellers who appreciate a gym on the road, families needing a three-person room, and couples drawn to the Eiffel Tower quarter. The medium cost level keeps comfort attainable without luxury pricing. Guests seeking spa indulgence should look at the high tier in Paris.

## Practical notes
Hotel Tour Eiffel Vue offers wifi, breakfast, ac and gym access at a medium cost level, with a maximum of three guests per room. Metro and river walks connect the area to the rest of Paris easily. The gym is handy for maintaining routines during longer stays, and booking ahead is wise for summer, when the tower district fills quickly.
