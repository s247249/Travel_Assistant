## Description
The Louvre Timed Entry & Highlights Tour is the classic introduction to museum-going in Paris. Housed in a former royal palace on the Right Bank of the Seine, the Louvre is the most visited museum in the world, and its sheer scale can overwhelm first-time visitors. This guided experience solves that problem: a reserved timed entry slot gets you through the glass pyramid without the longest queues, and a knowledgeable guide leads a focused route through the essential galleries. Expect the Mona Lisa, the Venus de Milo, the Winged Victory of Samothrace and the monumental French and Italian painting rooms, all placed in context with stories about the palace itself and the kings who built it. The visit lasts around three and a half hours, long enough to feel immersed but short enough to leave energy for the rest of Paris.

## Who it's for
This Paris tour suits couples and solo travellers who want depth rather than a checklist, and seniors who appreciate a paced, guided route through a vast building. Art lovers get the most from it, but curious first-timers benefit just as much. Families with small children may find the duration demanding; older teenagers who enjoy history usually do well.

## Practical notes
The Louvre Timed Entry & Highlights Tour runs daily in Paris and booking ahead is strongly recommended, especially in summer. Wear comfortable shoes, as the route covers considerable distance indoors. Large bags and luggage are not admitted, so travel light. The palace is easily reached by metro, and the surrounding arcades and gardens make a pleasant place to decompress afterwards. Cost is moderate for a guided museum experience.
