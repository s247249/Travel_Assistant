## Description
The Seine Evening Dinner Cruise combines French cuisine with the most romantic sightseeing route in Paris. Glass-walled boats glide along the Seine as dusk settles, passing under historic bridges and alongside illuminated monuments: Notre-Dame on its island, the long facade of the Louvre, the Musee d'Orsay in its former railway station, and finally the Eiffel Tower, which sparkles on the hour after nightfall. On board, a multi-course dinner showcases classic French cooking, typically with seasonal starters, a refined main course, cheese and dessert, accompanied by wines chosen to match. Service is unhurried and the atmosphere is calm rather than party-like, leaving plenty of room for conversation and photography through the panoramic windows. The full experience lasts about three hours, a leisurely floating evening that turns dinner itself into the sightseeing.

## Who it's for
This Paris cruise is ideal for couples marking an occasion, foodies who want French cuisine with a view, and seniors who prefer elegant sightseeing without walking. It also works for small family celebrations with well-behaved older children. Travellers on a tight budget or those prone to motion sensitivity should consider a simpler sightseeing cruise instead, as this is a premium, seated dining experience.

## Practical notes
The Seine Evening Dinner Cruise runs daily in Paris, and advance booking is essential in the summer season. Dress smart-casual; the mood on board is polished. Boats depart from central quays near major landmarks, easily reached by metro or a short taxi ride. Dietary requirements are best communicated when booking. Expect a premium cost level reflecting the dinner, wine and cruise combined, and allow time to arrive before departure for boarding.
