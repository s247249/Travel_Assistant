## Description
Hotel Jardin du Luxembourg is a comfortable mid-range retreat on the Left Bank of Paris, moments from the elegant gravel paths and green chairs of the Luxembourg Gardens. Rooms for up to two guests are calm and classically furnished, with wifi, a proper breakfast and air conditioning that makes summer stays restful. The location is the quiet star: mornings can begin with a stroll among the garden's fountains, and the Latin Quarter's bookshops, cafes and the Pantheon are an easy wander away. It is the kind of Paris address that suits travellers who want beauty and calm rather than nightlife at the door.

## Who it's for
This Paris hotel is ideal for couples, seniors and relax-seekers who value a peaceful, well-placed base with genuine comfort. Air conditioning and breakfast support unhurried summer mornings. Families needing rooms for three or four should consider larger mid-tier properties in Paris.

## Practical notes
Hotel Jardin du Luxembourg provides wifi, breakfast and ac at a medium cost level, with a maximum of two guests per room. The Left Bank setting means level, pleasant walking to gardens, museums and metro lines. Book ahead for the June-to-September season, when this quiet corner of Paris is quietly in demand.
