## Description
The Versailles Palace Day Trip carries visitors from Paris to the ultimate statement of royal power. Louis XIV transformed his father's hunting lodge into the Palace of Versailles, a residence so magnificent that Europe's monarchs spent a century imitating it. This full-day excursion covers the State Apartments, the dazzling Hall of Mirrors where the German Empire was proclaimed and the Great War's peace was signed, and the private chambers of kings and queens. Beyond the palace, the formal gardens stretch to the horizon in a geometry of fountains, parterres and clipped hedges, with the Grand Canal and the smaller estate of the Trianon palaces and Marie-Antoinette's rustic hamlet offering a quieter counterpoint. With transport from Paris handled and entrance arranged, the day unfolds over roughly six hours, blending architecture, court intrigue and landscape design into one grand outing.

## Who it's for
This day trip from Paris suits couples and seniors fascinated by history, and families with older children who can appreciate the scale and stories. Architecture and garden lovers will be in their element. It is a long day with substantial walking, so families with toddlers or visitors who prefer short outings should plan carefully or choose a gardens-focused visit.

## Practical notes
The Versailles Palace Day Trip runs on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays, and booking well ahead is strongly recommended for the summer months. Comfortable shoes are essential, as the gardens alone involve long distances. Bring water and sun protection for the outdoor sections, which offer little shade. The estate lies southwest of Paris and is reached by regional rail or excursion transport; this experience sits at a premium cost level reflecting transport, entry and guiding.
