## Description
Residence Marais Bleue is a family-friendly mid-range hotel in the heart of the Marais, one of the most walkable historic districts of Paris. With rooms for up to three guests and dedicated family rooms, it solves the classic Paris problem of finding space for a small family without jumping to luxury prices. Wifi, breakfast and air conditioning come standard, and the surrounding streets deliver everything within a short stroll: the arcaded Place des Vosges, museums in grand mansions, falafel counters, patisseries and boutiques. The district's medieval lanes survived the city's great rebuilding, so stepping outside feels like stepping into older Paris.

## Who it's for
Residence Marais Bleue suits families with one or two children and couples wanting extra space in central Paris. Family rooms and air conditioning make summer travel with kids far easier. Larger groups needing four in a room should look at the high-tier options in Paris.

## Practical notes
This Paris hotel lists wifi, breakfast, ac and family-rooms at a medium cost level, sleeping up to three per room. The Marais location keeps daily sightseeing on foot, with metro lines nearby for longer hops. Streets are narrow and occasionally cobbled, so a compact stroller helps. Reserve family rooms early for summer dates in Paris.
