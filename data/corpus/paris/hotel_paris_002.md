## Description
Maison Montmartre brings budget comfort to the artistic hill of Paris. Tucked into the lanes below Sacre-Coeur, this small guesthouse-style hotel offers simple rooms for up to two guests, wifi throughout and a modest breakfast of bread, pastries and coffee that sets guests up for the climb. Staying here means waking inside the village atmosphere that draws visitors to Montmartre: painters on the square, cobblestones, and some of the best rooftop views in Paris just minutes uphill. Evenings bring bistros and music spots rather than tourist bustle, and the metro below the hill connects easily to the rest of the city.

## Who it's for
Maison Montmartre suits young travellers, couples and solo visitors who want character and a low price in Paris. The included breakfast adds real value on a budget. Guests with mobility concerns should note the hill's slopes and steps, and those needing air conditioning should look elsewhere.

## Practical notes
This Paris hotel offers wifi and breakfast at a low cost level, with rooms for a maximum of two guests. The location rewards early mornings and late evenings, when Montmartre is at its most atmospheric. Expect compact rooms and a walk to the nearest metro; comfortable shoes are as useful here as anywhere in Paris.
