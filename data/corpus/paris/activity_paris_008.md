## Description
The Moulin Rouge Cabaret Show is the most famous night out in Paris, a spectacle of feathers, sequins and precision dance that has defined the city's idea of glamour since the Belle Epoque. Born at the foot of Montmartre in the late nineteenth century, the Moulin Rouge invented the can-can as a stage sensation and became the subject of Toulouse-Lautrec's most celebrated posters. Today's revue carries that legacy forward with elaborately costumed dance numbers, aerial and specialty acts, live singing and a big-band spirit, all staged in a plush red-and-gold auditorium under the windmill's turning sails. The show runs around two and a half hours including the atmosphere of arrival, and it delivers exactly what visitors hope for: a glittering, unapologetically theatrical Paris evening that feels like stepping into a poster from the 1890s.

## Who it's for
This Paris cabaret suits couples wanting a spectacular date night and young travellers looking for classic nightlife with a cultural pedigree. Fans of music, dance and costume design will be captivated. The revue includes partial nudity in the traditional cabaret style, so it is not appropriate for children, and families should choose other evening entertainment in Paris.

## Practical notes
The Moulin Rouge Cabaret Show takes place on Wednesdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, and booking ahead is essential, particularly in summer. A smart dress code applies; beachwear and sportswear are out of place. The venue sits in Pigalle at the base of Montmartre, served by metro. Seating is assigned, and arriving early helps the evening run smoothly. This is a premium-priced Paris experience, with optional dinner-and-show formats pushing the cost higher still. Photography of the performance itself is not permitted, so enjoy it live.
