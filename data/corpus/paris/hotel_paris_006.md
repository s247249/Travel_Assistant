## Description
Grand Palace Opera is the full-service luxury address of this Paris collection, set near the ornate Opera Garnier in the grand heart of the city. Rooms sleep up to four guests, with family rooms that let parents and children share elegant space, and the amenity list leaves nothing out: wifi, a refined breakfast, air conditioning, a spa for post-sightseeing recovery and a gym for keeping pace. The location places the department stores, covered passages and theatres of the opera district at the door, with the Louvre a comfortable walk away. Service is attentive and the atmosphere quietly grand, matching the high cost level.

## Who it's for
Grand Palace Opera suits families wanting luxury with room for four, couples on a milestone trip, relax-seekers who plan spa afternoons between museums, and seniors who value comfort and central convenience in Paris. Budget-focused travellers will find better value at the lower tiers.

## Practical notes
This Paris hotel provides wifi, breakfast, ac, spa, gym and family-rooms at a high cost level, with up to four guests per room. Spa treatments are best reserved ahead, especially in summer. The opera district is superbly connected by metro and walkable to major sights, making this a genuinely effortless Paris base.
