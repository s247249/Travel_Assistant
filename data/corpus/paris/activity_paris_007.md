## Description
The Marais Food & Wine Tasting Tour is a delicious education in the flavours of Paris, set in one of its most characterful districts. The Marais survived the great nineteenth-century rebuilding of the city, so its narrow streets are lined with aristocratic mansions, hidden courtyards and some of the oldest facades in Paris. A small-group guide leads tasters through this maze with stops at artisan fromageries, charcuterie counters, bakeries and specialist wine cellars. Expect aged French cheeses explained by region, cured meats, still-warm bread and pastries, and guided wine tastings that demystify French appellations and pairing habits. Between stops, the guide layers in the district's layered history: royal squares, medieval Jewish heritage and the Marais's modern life as a hub of galleries and boutiques. Over about three hours, the tastings add up to a generous, wandering meal.

## Who it's for
This Paris tasting tour is made for foodies and wine-curious couples, and for young travellers who want to understand French food culture beyond restaurant menus. It suits sociable people happy to walk, stand and chat in small groups. It is less suited to young children, and visitors with strict dietary restrictions should check compatibility when booking, as traditional tastings lean heavily on dairy, meat and wine.

## Practical notes
The Marais Food & Wine Tasting Tour runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays in Paris, and advance booking is recommended as groups are small. Come hungry and pace yourself across the stops. The route mixes shop interiors with street walking, so dress for the weather and wear comfortable shoes. This is a premium experience, but the volume of tastings makes it a substitute for a meal. Non-alcoholic alternatives can sometimes be arranged if requested ahead.
