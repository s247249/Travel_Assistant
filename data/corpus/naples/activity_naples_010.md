## Description
The Quartieri Spagnoli Street Art Tour explores the grid of steep, narrow lanes above Via Toledo, where the Spanish Quarter of Naples has become an open-air gallery. Giant murals honour local icons, from football legends to patron saints, alongside works by international street artists tucked between washing lines, scooters and shrines. A local guide reads the neighbourhood's codes: its working-class pride, its devotion and defiance, and how murals have reshaped its image while daily life carries on unchanged around them. The tour balances headline artworks with the small courtyards and artisan corners most visitors walk straight past.

## Who it's for
This Naples street art tour is ideal for young travellers and solo explorers interested in contemporary culture, and for couples who want the city's living neighbourhoods rather than only its monuments. Photographers of every age will find the light and layers irresistible.

## Practical notes
The Quartieri Spagnoli Street Art Tour runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, lasts about two hours and is moderately priced. The quarter is genuinely steep, with steps and slopes throughout, so wear grippy shoes. It is a lived-in neighbourhood: keep voices respectful in residential lanes.
