## Description
Hotel Lungomare Napoli is a mid-range family-friendly hotel on the seafront side of Naples, where dedicated family rooms sleep up to four and many windows frame the Gulf and Vesuvius. A buffet breakfast sets everyone up before the promenade, air conditioning keeps afternoons cool, and wifi covers the whole building. Strollers roll straight out onto the car-free Lungomare.

## Who it's for
This Naples hotel is designed for families who need real space and easy logistics, and it works equally well for couples or grandparents travelling with children who want sea views without luxury pricing.

## Practical notes
Hotel Lungomare Napoli is moderately priced, with family rooms worth reserving well ahead for summer. The flat seafront walk, nearby gardens and quick access to ferry terminals make day trips with children noticeably simpler.
