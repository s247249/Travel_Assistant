## Description
Grand Hotel Miramare is the premium address of this Naples collection, a full-service seafront hotel where panoramic sea-view rooms, an elegant breakfast and a small spa with treatments and a relaxation area define the stay. Family rooms sleep up to four without sacrificing style, air conditioning and fast wifi are standard, and staff arrange boats, tables and tours with quiet efficiency.

## Who it's for
The Grand Hotel Miramare suits couples celebrating something special, relax-seekers who want a spa day between excursions, and families seeking top-tier comfort with space for everyone.

## Practical notes
This Naples hotel sits at the high end of local pricing, with sea-view and family rooms in strongest demand during summer; reserve early. The spa is the natural recovery stop after Vesuvius hikes or long walking days in Pompeii.
