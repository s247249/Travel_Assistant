## Description
B&B Partenope is a small, friendly bed and breakfast in Naples near the seafront, run with the warmth of a family home. Simple, bright rooms keep the essentials done well, and a relaxed Italian breakfast of pastries, fruit and coffee starts the day without fuss. Reliable wifi throughout makes planning tomorrow's route easy over an evening espresso.

## Who it's for
This budget stay in Naples suits couples and solo travellers watching their spending, and young visitors who want a personal welcome rather than a front desk. Rooms sleep up to two guests.

## Practical notes
B&B Partenope is inexpensive by Naples standards and well placed for walks along the Lungomare and ferries from the nearby port areas. Breakfast and wifi are included; there is no air conditioning listed, so summer guests should favour the breezier rooms.
