## Description
The Castel dell'Ovo & Borgo Marinaro Visit explores the oldest castle of Naples, the "Egg Castle" perched on the islet of Megaride where, legend says, the poet Virgil hid a magical egg that protects the city. A short causeway leads from the shore to ramparts and terraces with sweeping views over the Gulf, Vesuvius and the marina below. Afterwards, the visit winds through Borgo Marinaro, the little former fishing village at the castle's feet, now a cluster of seafood restaurants and boat moorings where the rhythm of the harbour still shows through.

## Who it's for
This Naples visit suits families wanting a compact dose of history with sea views, couples planning a castle walk followed by a harbour dinner, and seniors who prefer short distances and rewarding panoramas over long museum corridors.

## Practical notes
The Castel dell'Ovo & Borgo Marinaro Visit can be made daily, takes around ninety minutes and is inexpensive. Access to the castle terraces involves ramps and steps rather than lifts, and occasional events may close sections temporarily. Pair it with the nearby Lungomare promenade; evenings in Borgo Marinaro are lively but relaxed.
