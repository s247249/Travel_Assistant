## Description
Hotel Chiaia Blu is a mid-range hotel in Naples' refined Chiaia district, where sea-view rooms look across the Gulf toward Vesuvius. Interiors are calm and contemporary, breakfast is a generous spread enjoyed near the windows, and air conditioning plus dependable wifi keep summer stays comfortable. The boutique streets and the seafront promenade are both a short stroll away.

## Who it's for
This Naples hotel is made for couples on a romantic break and relax-seekers who want quiet elegance within reach of the city's energy. Rooms host up to two guests, keeping the atmosphere intimate.

## Practical notes
Hotel Chiaia Blu is moderately priced for its sea views and location. Book a sea-view room early for summer dates, as they are the first to go. The Lungomare, beach clubs and Chiaia's aperitivo scene are all walkable from the door.
