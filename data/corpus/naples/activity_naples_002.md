## Description
The Spaccanapoli Old Town Walking Tour follows the straight ancient street that slices the historic centre of Naples in two, the axis that gave the neighbourhood its name. Along the way you pass baroque churches, hidden courtyards, artisan workshops making nativity figurines, shrines set into alley walls and the lively piazzas where daily Neapolitan life unfolds. The walk dips into side streets toward the Duomo and the San Gregorio Armeno lane, weaving two and a half thousand years of Greek and Roman layers into a compact, sensory route through one of Europe's most theatrical old towns.

## Who it's for
This Naples walking tour is ideal for young travellers and solo visitors who want to orient themselves quickly, couples who enjoy wandering with a story, and foodies eager to spot the pastry shops and pizzerias they will return to later. The pace is gentle, so most ages manage it comfortably.

## Practical notes
The Spaccanapoli Old Town Walking Tour operates daily, takes around two and a half hours and is inexpensive. Wear comfortable shoes for cobbles and expect scooters in narrow lanes. Mornings are calmer; late afternoons bring the fullest street life. Churches along the route may ask for covered shoulders.
