## Description
Residenza del Golfo is a mid-range residence-style hotel in Naples with views over the bay that give every morning a sense of occasion. Rooms are spacious by city standards and sleep up to three, with air conditioning, reliable wifi and a relaxed breakfast room where the day's plans get sketched over coffee. The feel is residential and unhurried rather than busy.

## Who it's for
This Naples stay suits couples wanting room to breathe, small families or three friends sharing, and seniors who value comfort, lift access and a calmer setting while staying near the seafront.

## Practical notes
Residenza del Golfo is moderately priced and a good base for combining city walks with boat departures to Capri and the coast. Sea-view rooms carry the best panoramas; request a higher floor when reserving for the widest outlook over the Gulf.
