## Description
The Pizza Margherita Masterclass in Naples is a hands-on lesson in the city's most famous invention. Working at your own bench with a pizzaiolo, you learn how Neapolitan dough is mixed and hand-stretched, why San Marzano tomatoes and fior di latte matter, and how the intense heat of a wood-fired oven produces the soft, leopard-spotted cornicione that defines the authentic style. Each participant shapes, tops and bakes their own pizza, then sits down to eat it with the group, comparing results and picking up tips to recreate the magic at home.

## Who it's for
This Naples masterclass is a natural fit for foodies and couples who like edible souvenirs, families with children old enough to handle dough safely, and young travellers travelling in groups who enjoy a sociable, hands-on break from sightseeing. No cooking experience is needed; enthusiasm and an appetite are enough.

## Practical notes
The Pizza Margherita Masterclass runs on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays and lasts about two and a half hours. It is moderately priced and booking ahead is recommended, as bench space is limited. The class is indoors and air-friendly in summer heat; mention dietary needs when reserving.
