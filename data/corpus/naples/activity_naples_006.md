## Description
The Lungomare Seafront Sunset Stroll follows the pedestrian promenade of Naples from the gardens of Villa Comunale through Via Caracciolo toward the fishing harbour of Borgo Marinaro. As the sun drops behind Posillipo, Vesuvius glows across the bay and Castel dell'Ovo turns amber on its islet. Locals jog, families share gelato on the benches, and fishing boats bob at their moorings. This free, unhurried walk is the city's daily ritual of decompression, offering the widest, most cinematic view of the Gulf of Naples without spending anything at all.

## Who it's for
This seafront stroll in Naples is perfect for couples wanting a romantic golden hour, seniors and families who appreciate flat, car-free walking, and relax-seekers who measure a day by its views rather than its monuments. It works equally well solo with a camera.

## Practical notes
The Lungomare Seafront Sunset Stroll can be done any day and costs nothing; allow about ninety minutes at an easy pace. The route is flat and stroller-friendly, with benches and kiosks along the way. Summer evenings are breezy and busy; arrive before sunset to claim a good railing spot.
