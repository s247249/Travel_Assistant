## Description
The Amalfi Coast & Positano Day Trip carries travellers from Naples around the Sorrento peninsula to the most celebrated coastline in Italy. Pastel villages cling to near-vertical cliffs above a turquoise sea, and the day centres on Positano, where cascading houses, boutique lanes and a pebble beach make every staircase a photo opportunity. Depending on the itinerary, there is time to admire Amalfi's striped cathedral and its maritime heritage or to pause at a panoramic belvedere above the fjord-like coves. The coastal drive itself, threading tunnels and hairpins between lemon terraces and the open sea, is half the experience.

## Who it's for
This day trip from Naples suits couples seeking postcard romance, young travellers chasing the classic coast, and relax-seekers content with views, lunches and a swim. Many steps and steep lanes make it demanding for strollers and for visitors who struggle with inclines.

## Practical notes
The Amalfi Coast & Positano Day Trip operates on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays, lasts about eight hours and is a premium excursion; book well ahead in high summer. Roads are slow and winding, so motion-sensitive travellers should prepare. Wear comfortable shoes and bring swim gear.
