## Description
The Pompeii Ruins Half-Day Trip takes visitors from Naples to the most famous archaeological site in Italy, the Roman city buried by the eruption of Vesuvius in 79 AD. Walking through the excavated streets, you see temples, bakeries, villas with faded frescoes and the forum framed by the volcano itself. A guided half-day format keeps the visit focused on the highlights, so the vast site never feels overwhelming, and the story of daily Roman life comes alive through mosaics, graffiti and casts preserved in ash.

## Who it's for
This trip from Naples suits families with older children who enjoy history, couples looking for a memorable shared experience, and seniors who prefer a guided pace with clear meeting points. Young travellers with an interest in archaeology will also find Pompeii endlessly rewarding, though very small children may tire of the long, uneven walks.

## Practical notes
The Pompeii Ruins Half-Day Trip runs daily and lasts about five hours including transfers from Naples. Booking ahead is recommended in summer. The site is largely exposed to the sun with little shade, so bring a hat, water and sturdy shoes; the ancient paving stones are uneven and slippery when wet.
