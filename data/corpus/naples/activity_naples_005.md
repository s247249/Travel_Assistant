## Description
The Napoli Sotterranea Underground Tour descends beneath the streets of Naples into a parallel city carved from the soft tufa rock. Greek quarrymen first hollowed these chambers, Romans turned them into cisterns for their aqueduct, and residents sheltered here during wartime air raids. Guided passages lead past vast water reservoirs, narrow candlelit corridors and traces of a Roman theatre, revealing how Naples has always grown on top of itself. It is an atmospheric, cool counterpoint to the sunlit chaos above and one of the most surprising experiences the city offers.

## Who it's for
The underground tour appeals to young travellers and solo visitors drawn to unusual history, and to families with adventurous children who enjoy tunnels and stories. Anyone uncomfortable in tight, dim spaces should skip the narrowest passages; seniors should consider the many steps carefully.

## Practical notes
The Napoli Sotterranea Underground Tour operates daily, lasts about two hours and is inexpensive. Booking is recommended as groups are small. Temperatures underground stay cool year-round, so a light layer helps in summer. Some sections involve steep stairs and passages barely shoulder-wide.
