## Description
The Capri & Blue Grotto Boat Day Trip sails from the port of Naples across the Bay to the island of Capri, circling dramatic cliffs, the Faraglioni rock stacks and hidden coves reachable only from the water. When sea conditions allow, small rowing boats carry passengers into the Blue Grotto, where sunlight filtering through an underwater opening turns the cave a surreal electric blue. The day includes free time to wander Capri town or Anacapri, swim from the boat in clear water and enjoy the island's glamorous lanes before the return crossing to Naples at golden hour.

## Who it's for
This boat day from Naples suits couples chasing romance, young travellers wanting an iconic island day, and relax-seekers happy to alternate sunbathing with gentle exploring. It is less suited to families with very small children or anyone prone to seasickness on open water.

## Practical notes
The Capri & Blue Grotto Boat Day Trip runs daily in season, lasts around eight hours and is a premium outing; book ahead in summer. The Blue Grotto visit depends on tide and wind and cannot be guaranteed. Bring swimwear, sunscreen and a light layer for the breeze.
