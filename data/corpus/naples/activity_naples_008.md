## Description
The Mount Vesuvius Crater Hike climbs the volcano that shaped the destiny of Naples and Pompeii. From the upper trailhead, a wide gravel path spirals steadily to the rim of the Gran Cono, where fumaroles still breathe sulphur into the air and the crater floor lies hundreds of metres below. Walking the rim, hikers take in a full panorama: the Bay of Naples curving beneath, the Sorrento peninsula, Capri on the horizon and the patchwork of towns living in the volcano's shadow. It is a moderate but sustained climb with a payoff few hikes in Europe can match.

## Who it's for
This hike near Naples is aimed at sporty travellers, young hikers and solo adventurers comfortable with a steady uphill on loose gravel. It is not ideal for families with small children or visitors with mobility or heart concerns; the altitude gain is modest but continuous.

## Practical notes
The Mount Vesuvius Crater Hike runs daily, lasts around four hours including transfers from Naples, and is moderately priced. Entry to the crater path is regulated, so booking ahead is wise in summer. There is no shade on the rim: bring water, sun protection and closed shoes; winds can be strong.
