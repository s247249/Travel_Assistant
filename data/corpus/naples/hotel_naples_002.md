## Description
Casa Spaccanapoli is a no-frills budget guesthouse in the beating heart of Naples' old town, steps from the famous straight street it is named after. Rooms are compact and practical, with air conditioning that makes summer nights restful despite the lively lanes below, and solid wifi for maps and messages. You are minutes from pizzerias, churches and street life.

## Who it's for
Casa Spaccanapoli fits young travellers and solo explorers who want to sleep in the middle of the action, plus small families or trios, since rooms accommodate up to three guests at low cost.

## Practical notes
This Naples guesthouse is inexpensive and unbeatable for walking the historic centre. Breakfast is not included, which locals consider a feature: the neighbourhood's bars and pastry shops are the real morning ritual. Expect some street noise on weekend evenings.
