## Description
The Gothic Quarter Evening Tapas Tour threads Barcelona's oldest streets together with a series of tasting stops. A local guide leads a small group through the labyrinth of the Barri Gotic, past Roman walls, hidden squares and the cathedral cloister, pausing at family-run bars and classic tapas counters. Expect the Catalan canon done properly: pan con tomate, croquetas, cured meats, grilled seafood and a sweet finish, each paired with regional wine, cava or vermouth. Between bites, the guide unpacks centuries of history and the rituals of the Spanish evening table.

## Who it's for
Foodies who want context with their tasting will get the most from this tour, and it is an easy win for couples looking for a sociable evening. Young travellers enjoy the bar-hopping rhythm without having to navigate menus in Catalan. It suits curious eaters more than strict diets, though many stops can adapt with notice.

## Practical notes
The tour runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays and lasts around three hours, entirely at walking pace through mostly pedestrian streets. Come hungry: the stops add up to a full dinner. Booking ahead is recommended as groups are kept small, and comfortable shoes matter on the old cobblestones of Barcelona's Gothic Quarter.
