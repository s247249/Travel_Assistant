## Description
Casa Barceloneta puts a budget stay steps from the sand in Barcelona's old fishermen's quarter. The guesthouse keeps things straightforward: tidy rooms for up to three guests, wifi throughout, air conditioning in every room, and select rooms with a sea view over the Mediterranean. Barceloneta itself is the attraction, a grid of narrow lanes hung with laundry, lined with seafood bars and opening directly onto the city's busiest beach. Morning swims before sightseeing become an easy habit here.

## Who it's for
Young beach-focused travellers, sporty visitors heading out for paddle sessions, and small families who want sand time without resort prices are the natural guests of Casa Barceloneta.

## Practical notes
Sea-view rooms are limited, so request one when reserving. The quarter is lively late into summer nights; earplugs help light sleepers. The beach, port promenade and metro into central Barcelona are all within a short walk.
