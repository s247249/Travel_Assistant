## Description
The Sagrada Familia Timed Entry & Towers experience brings you inside Antoni Gaudi's unfinished basilica, the defining symbol of Barcelona. With a reserved entry slot you step into a forest of branching columns washed in coloured light from the stained-glass windows, then ride a lift up one of the towers for close views of the sculpted spires and the orderly grid of the Eixample district below. The visit pairs the contemplative nave with the drama of the Nativity and Passion facades, making it the essential cultural stop in the city.

## Who it's for
This visit suits couples drawn to romantic architecture, families with older children who can manage the tower stairs, and seniors comfortable with moderate walking. Architecture lovers and photographers will find endless detail in every surface, while first-time visitors to Barcelona get the single most memorable sight of the trip in one focused visit.

## Practical notes
The experience runs daily and lasts around two hours, so reserve the timed entry well ahead during the summer months. Tower access involves narrow spiral stairways on the way down and is not suitable for very young children or anyone uneasy with heights. Dress modestly for a working basilica, and pair the visit with a stroll along the nearby Gaudi avenues of the Eixample.
