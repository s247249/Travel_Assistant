## Description
The Barceloneta Paddle Surf Lesson turns Barcelona's city beach into an open-air gym. After a shore briefing on stance, balance and safety, an instructor guides the group onto stable beginner boards in the usually gentle water off Barceloneta. Most first-timers are kneeling within minutes and standing by the end of the session, gliding along the shoreline with the skyline of the W hotel and the port cranes as a backdrop. Falling in is part of the fun, and the Mediterranean is warm enough in summer to make every splash welcome.

## Who it's for
Sporty travellers and young visitors looking for an active morning will love this lesson, and it works well for adventurous beginners with basic swimming skills. It is a sociable option for friends travelling together, and a refreshing counterpoint to Barcelona's museum-heavy sightseeing.

## Practical notes
Sessions run daily and last about two and a half hours including the briefing and time on the water. All equipment is provided; bring swimwear, a towel and sunscreen. Booking ahead is recommended in summer. The lesson depends on sea conditions, so expect a reschedule if the wind picks up, and avoid heavy meals immediately beforehand.
