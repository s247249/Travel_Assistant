## Description
The El Born Vermut & Cocktail Tasting introduces Barcelona's most cherished drinking ritual: la hora del vermut. Guided by a host through two characterful bars in El Born, the session starts with the Catalan classic itself, vermouth served over ice with an olive and orange slice, tasted side by side to reveal how botanicals, sweetness and bitterness differ between houses. Small snacks in the traditional style accompany the pours. The second half shifts gears to contemporary cocktails built on local spirits and vermouth bases, showing how Barcelona's bartenders reinterpret the aperitif for the modern night.

## Who it's for
Foodies and wine-curious travellers get a genuine slice of local culture, couples enjoy the unhurried, conversational format, and young travellers discover an alternative to club nights. It is ideal for anyone who wants to understand what Barcelonians actually drink before dinner.

## Practical notes
Tastings run on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays and last about two hours, seated or standing at the bar rather than walking far. Booking is recommended as groups stay small. The format includes alcohol throughout, so it is an adults-only experience. Arrive with an appetite for the snacks, and let the evening roll naturally into a late dinner nearby in El Born, Barcelona.
