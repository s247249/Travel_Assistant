## Description
Hotel Mirador Montjuic sits on the lower slopes of Montjuic hill in Barcelona, trading beach proximity for space, greenery and views. Rooms accommodate up to three guests with air conditioning and wifi, breakfast is included, and a well-equipped gym supports training routines on the road. The hill itself is the hotel's extended garden: running paths through parks, the cable car up to the castle, and the Olympic ring all nearby, while the metro and buses connect down to the port and the old town in minutes.

## Who it's for
Sporty travellers who want morning runs and a real gym, couples drawn to quieter evenings with a panorama, and families needing extra space at a mid-range level are the ideal guests.

## Practical notes
The hillside setting means some uphill walking; buses soften the climb. Breakfast is included and the gym suits early sessions before Barcelona's heat builds. Ask for a high-floor room facing the city for the best evening views over the port.
