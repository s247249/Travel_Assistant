## Description
Hostal Gracia Sol is a simple, cheerful budget hostal in Barcelona's Gracia neighbourhood, a village-like pocket of pedestrian squares and independent cafes north of the Eixample. Rooms are compact and bright, sleeping up to two guests, with reliable wifi and air conditioning that matters in the Barcelona summer. The surrounding streets supply everything the hostal keeps lean: bakeries for breakfast, vermouth bars for the evening, and metro links that reach the Gothic Quarter quickly.

## Who it's for
Young travellers and solo visitors prioritising location and value over facilities will feel at home, and couples on a tight budget get a well-connected base with genuine neighbourhood character.

## Practical notes
Reception can help with luggage storage and local tips. Rooms face either quiet inner patios or lively lanes, so light sleepers should ask for the patio side. Gracia's squares host evening gatherings in summer, part of the charm of staying here in Barcelona.
