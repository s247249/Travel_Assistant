## Description
Ciutadella Park Picnic & Rowboats is Barcelona's gentlest pleasure: an afternoon in the green heart of the city. Parc de la Ciutadella spreads out from the monumental fountain touched by the young Gaudi, past lawns shaded by palms, a boating lake and the grand facade of the Catalan parliament. The plan is simple: gather bread, cheese, fruit and cava from a nearby market, claim a patch of grass, and drift afterward in a rowboat on the lake where herons stalk the shallows. Buskers, bubbles and shade make the hours dissolve, and the park's paths link easily to El Born and the waterfront.

## Who it's for
Families get space for children to roam safely, couples find a low-cost romantic hour on the water, and relax-seekers recover from museum marathons under the trees. It is the right choice whenever the group needs a pause rather than another queue.

## Practical notes
The park is open daily and the picnic-and-rowboat combination fills about two hours, though nobody minds if it stretches. No booking is needed; rowboats are taken as they come. Bring a blanket and sun protection, and buy supplies before entering. In peak summer, aim for late afternoon when Barcelona's heat softens and the lake turns gold.
