## Description
Hotel Born Central is a mid-range boutique hotel tucked into the medieval streets of El Born, one of Barcelona's most atmospheric districts. Rooms for up to two guests combine stone-and-timber character with modern comfort: air conditioning, dependable wifi and a breakfast spread that leans Catalan, with good bread, tomato, local cheese and coffee done properly. Step outside and the Picasso Museum, Santa Maria del Mar and a dense cluster of tapas bars and cocktail spots are all within a few minutes' wander.

## Who it's for
Couples on a city break, solo travellers who want evenings on the doorstep, and foodies planning to eat their way through El Born will find the location hard to beat.

## Practical notes
The streets around the hotel are pedestrian, so taxis set down nearby rather than at the door. Breakfast is included, making mornings simple before museum visits. Evenings in this part of Barcelona run late; ask for a room away from the liveliest lanes.
