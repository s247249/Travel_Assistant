## Description
The Rooftop Bar Crawl strings together Barcelona's best-loved terrace evenings in one guided night. A host leads a small group between hand-picked rooftops around the Eixample and the old town, each with its own character: one facing the illuminated Sagrada Familia, another above the cathedral roofs, a third with a pool deck and DJ. Drinks are ordered at each stop while the group trades travel stories against a backdrop of the city glittering below. It is sightseeing and nightlife blended, with the skyline doing the work of a guidebook.

## Who it's for
Young travellers wanting a ready-made social night are the core audience, and couples on a city break enjoy the romance of watching Barcelona light up from above. It suits confident walkers who like a dressed-up but relaxed atmosphere rather than loud clubs.

## Practical notes
The crawl runs on Fridays and Saturdays and lasts around three hours, with short walks between venues. Booking ahead is recommended because terrace capacity is limited. Smart-casual dress helps at the sleeker rooftops, and each stop's drinks are settled as you go. Pace yourself: the summer night air in Barcelona makes the climbs between terraces feel effortless.
