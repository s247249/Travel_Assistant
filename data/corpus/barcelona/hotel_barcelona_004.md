## Description
Hotel Eixample Garden brings a resort touch to the middle of Barcelona, set on a calm street in the Eixample grid among Modernist facades. Rooms sleep up to three guests and come with air conditioning and wifi, breakfast is included, and the centrepiece is a garden pool where the city's heat loses its edge. After a morning at the Sagrada Familia or Casa Batllo, both reachable on foot or a short metro hop, returning to a shaded lounger feels like a second holiday stitched into the first.

## Who it's for
Families needing a midday reset, couples who mix sightseeing with downtime, and relax-seekers who measure a trip by pool hours will all be well served here.

## Practical notes
The pool is seasonal in character, at its best from late spring through early autumn, exactly the June to September window. Breakfast is included; request a garden-facing room for quiet. The Eixample location keeps most of central Barcelona within easy reach.
