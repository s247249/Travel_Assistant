## Description
Grand Hotel Passeig de Gracia is Barcelona's indulgent address on the city's grandest boulevard, surrounded by flagship stores and Gaudi landmarks. The high-tier property offers family rooms sleeping up to four guests, a rooftop pool with views along the avenue, and a spa with treatment rooms and thermal circuit for serious unwinding. Breakfast is a generous included affair, air conditioning and wifi are standard throughout, and the service style is polished without stiffness. Casa Batllo and La Pedrera are a short stroll away, making this luxury with the sightseeing built into the doorstep.

## Who it's for
Families wanting space and facilities in one booking, couples celebrating something special, and relax-seekers who plan spa afternoons between gallery visits will find the Grand Hotel Passeig de Gracia fits perfectly.

## Practical notes
Book family rooms and spa treatments ahead for summer stays. The rooftop pool is the prime spot in the June to September heat, busiest mid-afternoon. The boulevard location puts metro lines, shopping and Barcelona's Modernist route within immediate reach.
