## Description
Park Guell Timed Entry opens the gates to Gaudi's hillside garden city, one of Barcelona's most playful creations. The monumental core gathers the famous mosaic salamander on the stairway, the hypostyle hall of leaning columns, and the serpentine bench covered in trencadis tilework that curves around the main terrace. Beyond the ticketed zone, shaded pathways wind through viaducts and palm groves toward viewpoints that frame the whole city stretching down to the Mediterranean. It is architecture and parkland fused into a single open-air wander.

## Who it's for
Families appreciate the open spaces where children can burn energy between photo stops, couples enjoy the terrace at the softer light of late afternoon, and young travellers come for the iconic images. It also suits anyone who prefers sightseeing outdoors rather than in museums, though the slopes mean a reasonable level of fitness helps.

## Practical notes
Park Guell operates daily and a visit takes about two hours including the free surrounding paths. Timed entry controls numbers in the monumental zone, so booking ahead is wise in high season. The park sits on a hill in the Gracia side of Barcelona: wear comfortable shoes, bring water in summer, and seek the shaded upper walks when the midday sun is strong.
