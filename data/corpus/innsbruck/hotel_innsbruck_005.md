## Description
Alpenhotel Talstation is a mid-range hotel positioned near the valley transport links of Innsbruck, built with active families in mind. Dedicated family-rooms sleep up to four guests, with practical layouts that give parents and children their own corners. Wifi is reliable, the breakfast buffet keeps everyone fuelled with cereals, eggs, bread and regional produce, and the sauna offers grown-ups a quiet hour after the children wind down. A ski-storage room with boot warmers serves winter visits, while in summer the same space handles bikes and hiking packs. The cable car and bus connections nearby put both the mountains and Innsbruck's old town within easy reach.

## Who it's for
Families are the headline audience thanks to the four-person rooms, while sporty travellers and young groups value the gear storage and quick access to lifts and trails around Innsbruck.

## Practical notes
At a medium cost level, Alpenhotel Talstation offers strong value for groups of four. The location favours mountain access over old-town atmosphere; evenings in central Innsbruck mean a short ride back.
