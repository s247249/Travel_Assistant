## Description
Hotel Bergblick Innsbruck is a comfortable mid-range hotel on a hillside shoulder of the city, where many rooms live up to the name with views over Innsbruck to the Nordkette. Interiors lean modern-Tyrolean: light wood, wool textiles and clean lines. Rooms sleep up to three guests, wifi is fast throughout, and the breakfast buffet leans regional, with Tyrolean speck, mountain cheese and good bread. After active days, the sauna is a welcome ritual, and a heated ski-storage room with boot dryers keeps gear ready for winter mountain days.

## Who it's for
Sporty travellers use it as a base for hiking and biking around Innsbruck, couples come for the views and the sauna evenings, and young travellers find the mid-range pricing fair for the comfort offered.

## Practical notes
The hillside setting means a walk downhill to the centre and a climb back, or a short bus hop. There is no on-site parking, so rail arrivals suit this hotel best. At a medium cost level, Hotel Bergblick Innsbruck balances comfort and value well.
