## Description
Haus Innbrucke is a practical budget guesthouse on the edge of Innsbruck, favoured by travellers arriving by car. Rooms are plainly furnished but comfortable, sleeping up to three guests, which makes them workable for small families or a trio of friends. Wifi reaches every room, a straightforward breakfast of bread, jams, cereal and hot drinks is included, and free on-site parking removes the usual headache of visiting Innsbruck with a vehicle. Bus stops nearby connect the house to the old town and the cable car stations in a short ride.

## Who it's for
Families watching their spending, young road-trippers and sporty travellers hauling bikes or hiking gear appreciate the space, parking and easy access to the roads out of Innsbruck toward the valleys.

## Practical notes
The location is residential rather than central, so plan on a bus ride or a longer walk to Innsbruck's sights. Rooms cap at three guests; larger groups need multiple rooms. At the low cost level, Haus Innbrucke trades polish for practicality, and does so honestly.
