## Description
The Old Town & Golden Roof Walking Tour is the classic introduction to Innsbruck, threading through the compact historic centre at the foot of the Nordkette. The route centres on the Goldenes Dachl, the late-medieval oriel with its gilded copper tiles that Emperor Maximilian I commissioned around 1500, still the city's most recognisable landmark. From there the walk winds past the colourful townhouses of Herzog-Friedrich-Strasse, the arcaded lanes, the Court Church with its imperial monuments, and the elegant Maria-Theresien-Strasse with the Anna Column framing the mountains. A local guide connects the sights with stories of Habsburg rule, trade routes over the Brenner Pass, and the daily life of Innsbruck then and now.

## Who it's for
This gentle two-hour walk suits almost everyone: couples and seniors appreciate the flat, compact route, solo travellers get oriented quickly, and families find the compact old town manageable with children.

## Practical notes
The tour runs daily in Innsbruck and is inexpensive, with no advance booking normally needed outside peak periods. Wear comfortable shoes for cobbled streets. Mornings are quieter in the old town; in August the lanes around the Golden Roof get busy, so an early slot is more pleasant.
