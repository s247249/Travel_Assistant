## Description
The Nordkette Cable Car to Hafelekar is the signature mountain experience of Innsbruck, lifting visitors from the edge of the old town to the high alpine ridge in a matter of minutes. The journey combines a funicular and two cable car stages, passing the Alpenzoo and the Seegrube station before reaching the Hafelekar summit area above two thousand metres. From the top, Innsbruck spreads out along the Inn valley below, while the wild limestone peaks of the Karwendel range rise behind. Short marked paths lead to viewpoints in every direction, and on clear days the panorama stretches deep into the Alps.

## Who it's for
This outing suits sporty travellers who want quick access to high terrain, couples chasing a memorable panorama, and young travellers looking for a dramatic photo stop without a full day of climbing. Families with older children manage it well too, though the exposed summit paths need supervision.

## Practical notes
The Nordkette runs daily and departures are frequent, but booking ahead is recommended in summer. Allow around four hours for the full round trip from Innsbruck, including time at the top. Temperatures at Hafelekar are far cooler than in the city, so bring layers and sturdy shoes even in July. Weather can turn quickly; check summit visibility before ascending.
