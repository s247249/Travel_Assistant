## Description
The Inn River Promenade & Sunset Picnic is the simplest pleasure Innsbruck offers: a slow stroll along the Inn as the day winds down, finished with a picnic on the grassy banks. The riverside paths run past the pastel row of houses at Mariahilf, one of the city's most photographed fronts, with the Nordkette glowing above the rooftops in the evening light. Cyclists, joggers and families share the wide promenade, benches appear at regular intervals, and grassy strips invite you to spread a blanket. Stock up beforehand on speck, mountain cheese, bread and fruit from an Innsbruck market or deli, and watch the mountains turn pink as the sun drops behind the western peaks.

## Who it's for
Couples get a romantic, no-cost evening; families appreciate flat paths and open space for children; relax-seekers and solo travellers can simply sit and watch the river. Everyone, regardless of budget or fitness, can enjoy this side of Innsbruck.

## Practical notes
This is free and available daily, with no booking of any kind. Allow about two hours around sunset. The promenade is flat and paved, suitable for strollers. Shops in Innsbruck close relatively early, so buy picnic supplies in the afternoon, and bring a light layer for riverside evenings.
