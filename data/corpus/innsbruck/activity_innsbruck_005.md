## Description
The Mountain Bike Downhill Intro Session is Innsbruck's adrenaline option, a coached first taste of gravity riding on designated beginner terrain in the mountains around the city. Innsbruck has a serious mountain biking culture, and this session channels it safely: after a skills briefing on body position, braking and line choice, an instructor leads riders onto flowing, forgiving trails with rollable features. Full-suspension bikes and protective gear are provided, and uplifts or cable car access handle the climbing so the session focuses on descending. By the end, most riders link corners confidently and understand how to progress in the local bike park scene.

## Who it's for
This is squarely for sporty travellers and young thrill-seekers with reasonable fitness and general bike confidence. It is not suited to families with children, seniors, or anyone uneasy on two wheels; complete beginners to cycling should choose a different activity in Innsbruck.

## Practical notes
Sessions run on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, and booking is essential because group sizes and equipment are limited. The premium cost level reflects coaching, bike hire and protection. Plan for about four hours. Wear sturdy shoes and sportswear; the instructor adjusts terrain to conditions and rider level.
