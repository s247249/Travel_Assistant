## Description
The Alpenzoo Alpine Zoo Visit is one of Innsbruck's most distinctive attractions: a zoo perched on a steep forested slope above the Inn river, devoted entirely to the animals of the Alps. Founded with the idea of showing alpine wildlife in naturalistic settings, it houses ibex, chamois, marmots, golden eagles, bearded vultures, brown bears, wolves, lynx and otters, along with farm breeds from Tyrolean valleys. Terraced paths climb through the enclosures, so a visit doubles as a pleasant woodland walk with ever-changing views over Innsbruck's rooftops to the mountains beyond. Interpretive boards explain alpine ecology and reintroduction programmes, giving the visit real substance beyond animal-spotting.

## Who it's for
Families are the prime audience: children meet animals they might glimpse in the wild, and the compact site suits shorter legs. Seniors and couples enjoy the shaded paths and views, though the slope means some steady climbing.

## Practical notes
The Alpenzoo opens daily and entry is inexpensive; no booking is needed. Allow around two and a half hours. From Innsbruck's old town you can walk up in about half an hour or take the Hungerburg funicular to the nearby station. Wear comfortable shoes for the terraced paths.
