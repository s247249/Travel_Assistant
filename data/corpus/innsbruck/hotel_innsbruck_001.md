## Description
Pension Alpenrose is a small, family-run budget guesthouse in a quiet residential lane within walking distance of Innsbruck's old town. Rooms are simple and spotless, with wooden furniture, firm beds and alpine prints on the walls, and each accommodates up to two guests. Reliable wifi covers the house, and the included breakfast is a homely spread of bread rolls, cold cuts, cheese, muesli and coffee served in a cosy tiled parlour. The hosts know Innsbruck well and happily sketch out bus routes and walking shortcuts to the Nordkette funicular.

## Who it's for
Couples and solo travellers on a tight budget are the natural fit, as are young travellers who plan to spend their days outdoors and need only a clean, friendly base in Innsbruck.

## Practical notes
With rooms for a maximum of two, this is not a family option. There is no parking or wellness area; guests arrive on foot or by public transport. The low cost level and included breakfast make Pension Alpenrose one of the better-value bases for exploring Innsbruck.
