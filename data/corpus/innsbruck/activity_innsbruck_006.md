## Description
The Bergisel Ski Jump & Panorama Walk brings visitors face to face with one of the most striking pieces of sports architecture in the Alps. Rising above the southern edge of Innsbruck, the Bergisel tower was designed by Zaha Hadid and combines a working ski jump with a public panorama platform and cafe. A funicular carries visitors up the tower to the viewing level, where the in-run drops away vertiginously below and the whole Inn valley opens up, framed by the Nordkette and the peaks toward the Brenner. The site is a stage of the famous Four Hills Tournament and hosted Olympic competitions, and displays tell that story. A short signed walk around the hillside adds history panels and views over the city.

## Who it's for
Sporty travellers love the venue's competitive heritage, young visitors come for the architecture and the photos, and families find the funicular and platform an easy, exciting outing without real effort.

## Practical notes
Bergisel is open daily and inexpensive, with no booking needed for standard visits. Allow about two hours including the panorama walk. The site is reachable from central Innsbruck by tram or a moderate uphill walk. The platform is exposed, so bring a layer on windy days.
